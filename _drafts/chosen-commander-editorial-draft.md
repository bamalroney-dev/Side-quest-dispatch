# Jagex proposes post-quest activities for The Chosen Commander

**Side Quest Dispatch · Gaming / Old School RuneScape · Editorial review draft · November 28, 2026**

Jagex says it expects to open a player poll the week of November 30 on proposed activities and rewards tied to *The Chosen Commander* in *Old School RuneScape*. Early 2027 is a tentative target for Dorgesh-Kaan. [Jagex’s November 27 poll blog update](https://secure.runescape.com/m=news/the-chosen-commander---post-quest-activities-poll-blog?oldschool=1)

The proposals include a Throne Room Agility course, campfires that would restore run energy, and a Gasworks activity that would train Crafting and Firemaking. Jagex also proposes a six-dose potion flask and a separate poll question on an extra Slayer block slot at 350 quest points. [Jagex’s proposed poll questions](https://secure.runescape.com/m=news/the-chosen-commander---post-quest-activities-poll-blog?oldschool=1)
